I'm a **PhD candidate** at the [School of Artificial Intelligence, CUHK-Shenzhen](https://sai.cuhk.edu.cn/en/), advised by [Prof. Feng Yin](https://sai.cuhk.edu.cn/en/teacher/97) and [Prof. Tsung-Hui Chang](https://sai.cuhk.edu.cn/en/teacher/83). In summer 2026, I was a research intern at [ByteDance (Douyin)](https://www.bytedance.com/en/), working on generative recommendation and LLM4Rec. Before that, I interned at at [Huawei](https://www.huawei.com/en), where I worked on [scalable black-box optimization](https://richardcsuwandi.github.io/papers/zap/) for 5G systems, and at [Dria](https://dria.co/), where I worked on [evolutionary coding agents](https://richardcsuwandi.github.io/projects/openevolve/) for algorithm discovery and [codebase optimization](https://richardcsuwandi.github.io/projects/kai/). I also co-founded the [Institute for AI-Driven Discovery of Algorithms (AIDDA)](https://algorithmdiscovery.org/), a community and research network focused on AI-driven research systems.

I did my undergraduate degree in Statistics (with first-class honors) at CUHK-Shenzhen, where I got into ML/AI research. In summer 2021, I worked as a research assistant at the [Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data (SRIBD)](https://www.sribd.cn/en), developing [federated matrix factorization](https://richardcsuwandi.github.io/papers/fedmavg/) for large-scale recommender systems.

My research focuses on [scalable and adaptive learning for optimization and discovery](https://richardcsuwandi.github.io/research/). Here are some core problems I'm thinking about:
- How can models adapt their assumptions to the task when data and evaluations are limited?
- How can AI agents discover new algorithms and models that we can verify?
- How do we evaluate AI research systems on real problems and improve them from feedback?

If you want to talk about research or collaborations, feel free to [reach out](mailto:richardcsuwandi@link.cuhk.edu.cn). 

Besides research, I'm also a dev ambassador at [Qwen](https://qwen.ai/ambassador) (Alibaba Cloud) and a community member at [AIXI Labs](https://www.aixi.uk/). I also previously led the AI4Science community at [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/).
